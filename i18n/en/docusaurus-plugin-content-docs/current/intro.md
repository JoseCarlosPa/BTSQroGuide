# Welcome to BTS Querétaro Guide

Hello! Welcome to the centralized documentation and best practices hub for the **Querétaro Office**.

---

## 🎯 Purpose of this Portal

The main objective of this portal is to serve as the central meeting and consultation point for the entire Querétaro team:

- **Centralization**: Provide a single, accessible, and up-to-date place to consult how we work.
- **CMMI Alignment**: Serve as the foundation to gradually document and standardize our processes under the CMMI maturity model.
- **Culture and Operations**: Facilitate team integration and day-to-day operational queries.
